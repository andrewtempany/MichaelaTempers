## Design Context

### Users
Music fans, festival bookers, venue promoters, and press/media in New Zealand and Australia. They arrive via social links, Spotify artist pages, or recommendations from other gig-goers. Music fans want to feel a connection to the artist and find out when/where to see her live. Industry contacts (bookers, press) need quick access to bio, photos, and booking info. They're often browsing on mobile, at a show, or in a noisy office.

### Brand Personality
Warm, grounded, handcrafted, intimate, confident but not flashy. Three words: **tender**, **rooted**, **unhurried**.

Michaela is a storyteller first. The site should feel like the inside of a well-worn record sleeve — not a streaming platform, not a SaaS product page, not a music blog.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Theme:** Light. Cream backgrounds, natural warmth. This is a daytime/living room experience — someone putting a record on a Sunday morning, not a late-night streaming dashboard.

**Visual tone:** Editorial folk. Think indie record label websites, letterpress gig posters, hand-stamped zines, folk festival programs. The kind of design that looks intentional and slightly analog even on a screen.

**Palette (fixed):**
- `--color-primary: #C75B4F` — terracotta/rust (CTAs, accents, active states)
- `--color-secondary: #2D5A5A` — deep teal (secondary accent, footer)
- `--color-accent: #D4A574` — soft gold (focus rings, blockquote borders)
- `--color-background: #FAF7F2` — cream (page background)
- `--color-text: #2C2C2C` — charcoal (body text)
- `--color-text-light: #5A5A5A` — secondary text
- `--color-border: #E5DFD6` — warm light border

**Typography (fixed):**
- Headings: Damona (custom, brand typeface — never changes)
- Body: Montserrat Thin (weight 100, needs room to breathe)

**References:** Album liner notes, indie record label sites (like Dead Oceans, Bella Union), letterpress poster aesthetics, folk festival programs, NZ handcraft aesthetic.

**Anti-references:** Corporate music industry sites, Spotify/Apple Music UI patterns, SaaS dashboards, generic template sites, tech-startup aesthetics, anything with gradient text or purple.

### Design Principles

1. **Print-inspired, not screen-native.** Every layout decision should look like it could exist as a physical object — a poster, a programme, a record sleeve. No glassmorphism, no shadows, no blur.

2. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** Damona headings are the visual signature. Size, weight, and spacing create hierarchy — not color blocks or cards.

3. **Warmth through restraint.** Terracotta and teal are used sparingly. Cream is the canvas. Colour draws the eye to exactly one thing at a time.

4. **No cards, no boxes.** Content sits on the page directly. Sections are separated by whitespace and hairline rules, not containers with backgrounds and shadows.

5. **Surprise without noise.** Creative features (music player, lyric callouts, countdowns) feel like things Michaela herself would put on a site she made by hand — not a feature checklist from a web agency.

### Known Issues to Fix
- `.hero` uses `linear-gradient()` — must be replaced with solid colour or texture
- Shadows used throughout — remove, replace with whitespace/border contrast
- Base font-size 16px — bump to 18px minimum
- Rounded corners on cards — reduce toward editorial sharpness
- `lang="en"` on `<html>` — should be `lang="en-NZ"`

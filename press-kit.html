Artist Bio

Michaela Tempers is a New Zealand folk-roots artist whose intimate storytelling and lush organic arrangements have captivated audiences across Aotearoa. Her debut EP "Good Woman" showcases a distinctive voice that blends traditional folk sensibilities with contemporary indie influences.

Drawing from personal experiences and universal themes of love, loss, and resilience, Michaela creates soundscapes that feel both timeless and fresh. Her performances are marked by emotional depth and authentic connection with audiences.

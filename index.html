Michaela Tempers

Folk-roots storyteller from Aotearoa

About

Michaela Tempers is a New Zealand-based folk-roots artist whose music weaves together intimate storytelling with lush, organic arrangements. Her debut EP "Good Woman" showcases her distinctive voice and poetic lyricism, drawing listeners into worlds both personal and universal.

With influences ranging from traditional folk to contemporary indie, Michaela creates soundscapes that feel both timeless and fresh. Her songs explore themes of love, loss, resilience, and the quiet moments that shape our lives.

Since emerging on the New Zealand music scene, Michaela has shared stages with acclaimed artists including Creedence Clearwater Revisited, Boy & Bear, and Holly Arrowsmith, captivating audiences with her authentic performances and emotional depth.
